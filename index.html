2. Pronunciation & Word Stress Gym
Word Stress Rule #1
The "Analyze" Family
Verb: AN-a-lyze
Noun: a-NAL-y-sis
Adj: an-a-LYT-i-cal

Notice how the primary stress shifts with each grammatical form!

Consonant Drill
/w/ vs /v/ Sound

/w/: Round lips like blowing a candle (never touch teeth).

"We work with warehouse workflows."

Tip: Check the mirror. If top teeth touch the bottom lip, it will sound like /v/.

Intonation & Flow
Natural Fillers (No "E-e-e")
"Looking at the figures..."
"Essentially, what happened is..."
"To put this into context..."

Use these natural transition phrases when you need 2 seconds to think!

3. Grammar & Slavic L1 Transfer Polish
Articles with Technical Nouns

In Russian, articles don't exist. In English, singular countable tech nouns need an article:

"We launched dashboard yesterday."
"We launched a new dashboard yesterday."
"I pushed the fix to the production cluster."
Future Intentions vs. Ongoing State

Avoid "I going to" without the auxiliary verb:

"I going to search in few months."
"I am planning to interview in a few months."
"I intend to start applying by November."

Assignment 1: The 90-Second Executive Voice Pitch

Estimated time: 15–20 minutes
1 The Scenario & Data Prompt

Imagine you are in a Monday morning product sync with your Head of Product and Senior Engineering Manager. You are presenting the latest data insight:

📊 The Metric Observation:
• Metric: Mobile onboarding completion rate dropped from 74% to 61% last week.
• Cause: Latency in the SMS two-factor authentication verification API.
• Recommendation: Switch to WhatsApp backup delivery or increase API timeout threshold.
2 Your Objective

Record a 60 to 90-second voice message (on Preply or Telegram) or a quick Loom video addressing the team. You must apply the 3-step formula:

  • The What: Introduce the 13% drop-off clearly without rambling.
  • The So What: Explain the business risk (user frustration, lower weekly active users).
  • The Now What: Propose the engineering fix with confident advisory phrasing (*"I recommend...", "To mitigate this..."*).
3 Pronunciation Checklist (Self-Audit Before Sending)
Check 1 Did you say AN-a-lyze (verb) and a-NAL-y-sis (noun) with correct syllable stress?
Check 2 Did you round your lips for we, work, and warehouse without touching teeth?
Check 3 Did you eliminate "e-e-e" silence by using "Looking at..." or "Essentially..."?
